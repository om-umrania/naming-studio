# Method and attribution

The starting skill was adapted from David Placek's interview on Lenny's Podcast, June 29, 2025. Original source notes were not supplied. Consult the original interview before attributing additional claims or case studies to him. This plugin is an independent adaptation; it does not claim affiliation with or endorsement by Placek, Lexicon, or Lenny's Podcast.

The identify–invent–implement process and diamond exercise inform the workflow. Output sizes, candidate ledgers, screening labels, skill boundaries, project files, and routing are practical assistant-workflow adaptations, not Placek's prescriptions.

The source skill reports Placek describing thousands of ideas and directions, not thousands of finished names. Never imply generation that did not occur. It also reports his associations of V with liveliness, B with reliability, Z with attention, and X with crispness and innovation as claims about Lexicon's research. They are optional creative prompts, not universal linguistic rules. Assess whole words in the intended languages. His views on .com and AI search are perspectives, not verified current market facts.
